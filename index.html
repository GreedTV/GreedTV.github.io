    How to play

    1. 3 vs 3. You and 2 teammates (blue) against the red team.
    2. Hit to push. Every hit knocks the target back and fills their strain bar. The fuller it is, the further they fly.
    3. Take zones. Knock all three enemies back across a line and your team takes that zone. Anyone knocked over a line can't walk back past it.
    4. Ring out. Push someone off the back edge into the water and they're out for the round.
    5. Ultimate. Landing hits charges your ultimate. When it glows, unleash it: Inferno, Tsunami, Earthquake or Tornado.
    6. Win. Knock out the whole team, or hold more ground when time runs out. First to 2 rounds wins.

    🌐 Play online

    Up to 6 players, 3 per team. Empty spots are filled with computer fighters. Players connect directly; a free public matchmaking service introduces them.
